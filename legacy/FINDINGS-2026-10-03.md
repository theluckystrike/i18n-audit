# i18n-hunt summary (2026-10-03)

Tool. `i18n_audit.py` generalizes medusa-proof/audit.py. It handles i18next `{{x}}` and `$t()`, ICU / Lingui / Tolgee / react-intl `{x}` with a real plural/select parser (branch-only args and quoted `'{x}'` handled), Rails `%{x}`, printf `%s`, Rocket.Chat `__x__`, `${x}` template vars and `<0>` / `<b>` tags. Kinds are syntax, renamed, dropped, extra, broken, mixed, tag, plural_count. "Real" means everything except tag and plural_count. Per-repo rows are in `audit-<repo>.tsv`. `remaining.py` drops keys that other open PRs touch (their diffs are in `prdiffs/`).
